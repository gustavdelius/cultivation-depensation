Interactive companions to the paper

Why fishing the prey can save the predator

Big predatory fish keep down the small fish that would otherwise eat their young. Overfish the predators and the small fish take over, and then a fishing ban alone will not bring the predators back. These two apps run a size-spectrum model of that community in your browser, so you can try it for yourself.

The Nursery Trap

For fishery managers and anyone new to the idea. No background needed.

  • Five short missions, from finding the edge of safe fishing to rebuilding a collapsed sea
  • A live sea, a logbook and a policy chart beside the fishing levers
  • A guided tour on first visit; works on phones

Runs the paper's mizer model exactly, ported line by line to JavaScript.

  • Guided scenarios and an auto-played 570-year story

  • Interventions such as seeding cohorts or a storm shock, and live telemetry including the predator's invasion number

    What is going on under the surface

    CULTIVATION

    Adult predators tend their own nursery

    Predators like cod eat forage fish like herring and sprat. By keeping forage fish scarce, the adults make the sea safe for their own larvae, which start life at one milligram, small enough to be eaten by a forage fish.

    DEPENSATION

    Remove the adults and the nursery turns hostile

    Fish the adults down and forage fish multiply, up to 24 times their usual abundance. They eat the predator larvae: danger in the nursery rises from about 1 to almost 40 deaths per larva per year. Too few young survive to replace their parents, and the decline feeds itself.

    RESTORATION

    Fishing the prey reopens the door

    A fishing ban does not remove the forage fish, so the nursery stays hostile and predators cannot return. Fishing the forage fish does the adults' old job. Once the predators are back, they take over and the forage fishery can stop again.

    About the model

    The simulation runs, live in your browser, the three-group size-spectrum model of the accompanying paper (built with the mizer R package): a plankton resource, a forage fish that grows to 20 g and a predator that grows to 2 kg. Both fish species hatch at 1 mg, and who eats whom is set by body size, so roles reverse as the predator grows up.

    • Every number you see is computed by the model as you play. Nothing is scripted. The engine is a line-by-line port of mizer's equations and reproduces mizer's own simulations to about 15 significant digits.
    • Fishing pressure F is the yearly fishing mortality rate. The percentage beside it is the share of catchable fish removed per year. Predators are caught from 400 g, forage fish from 1 g.
    • Stock sizes are model biomasses, shown as thousand tonnes (kt) to make them easier to read. Compare them with each other and with the healthy level, not with any real fishery.
    • The comeback number is how many offspring a predator would leave over its lifetime if predators were rare in the current sea. Above 1 a small population grows. Below 1 it shrinks, however few are caught.
